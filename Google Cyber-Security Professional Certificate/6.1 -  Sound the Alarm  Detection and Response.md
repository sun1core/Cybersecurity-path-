#### Ongoing monitoring of CI/CD: Automatically finding threats
##### Common Indicators of Compromise ( IoCs) in CI/CD pipelines;
- unauthorized code changes
- suspicious deployment patterns
- compromised dependencies
- unusual pipeline execution 
- secret exposure attempts

##### Proactive security through monitoring for IoCs
- indicators before serious damage occurs
- respond to incident quickly 
- limit the damage
- Improve threat knowledge 

##### Using automation to find anomalies and IoCs
1. Comprehensive logging and auditing
- pipeline execution logs
- code commit logs
- deployment logs

1. Security Information and Event Management ( SIEM ) Integration
- automatically find anomalies
- use rules to alert for known IoCs

1. Real-time altering and notifications
- unusual build failure 
- suspicious code changes ( Based on Anomalies )
- Attempt to expose secrets
- unusual network traffic

###### Triage
- propitiating of incidents according according to their level of importance or urgency

##### Triage of Process:
- receive & Processes 
- assign priority
- collect & analyze

##### Questions to ask:
1. is there is anything out of the ordinary?
2. Are there multiple failed login attempts?
3. Did the login happen outside of normal working hours?
4. Did login happen outside of the network?

##### Benefits of triage

#### Containment, eradication & recovery phase of the lifecycle

###### Containment
- the act of limiting and preventing additional damage caused by incident

###### Eradication 
- complete removal of the incident elements from all affected systems.

###### Recovery
- process of returning affected systems back to normal operations
